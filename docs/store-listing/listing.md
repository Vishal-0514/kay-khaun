## Full description (max 4000)

```
"Kya khaun?" – the question every Indian asks three times a day. Now you have someone to ask.

Chatora, your food buddy in the app, helps you decide in seconds. Tell it what you feel like, or just tap a card.

COOK AT HOME
• Tell Chatora what's in your kitchen, or snap your fridge, and get recipes you can make right now.
• Cooking mode reads each step aloud. Say "next" or "repeat" with messy hands, or switch the voice off.
• Finished? Keep a photo of your dish in My Kitchen and unlock cooking achievements. Photos stay on your phone.

ORDER ONLINE
• Chatora asks what you're craving, veg or non-veg, and your budget, then suggests dishes and restaurants near you.
• Open the restaurant's page on Zomato or Swiggy in one tap, or call the restaurant.

GO OUT
• Cafés and restaurants worth the drive, with travel times, for a date, friends or family.

FESTIVAL FOOD
• What people eat for each festival – Navratri, Diwali, Holi, Eid and more – and where to find it near you.

MORE WAYS TO PICK
• Snap Your Order: take a photo of where you are and get dishes that fit the moment.
• Order for a Group: tell us how many people, how many are veg and the budget each.
• Plan My Day: breakfast to dinner within a budget.
• Songs for your meal: snap your food and get songs to match.

MADE FOR INDIA
• English and Hindi.
• Talk instead of typing.
• Veg and non-veg marked clearly. Sweets are always veg.

PRIVACY
• No ads. We don't sell your data.
• No taste profile: Chatora asks when it needs to know.
• Delete your account and all your data any time, right in the app.

Chatora's suggestions are made with AI (Claude by Anthropic) and the app asks your permission first. Kya Khaun? doesn't sell food or take payments, and isn't affiliated with Zomato or Swiggy.

Made by KARIS GLOBAL RETAIL PRIVATE LIMITED.
```
