# Food photos for Kya Khaun: prompts

Make each picture in ChatGPT (image generation) or Google Gemini, then save it with the **file name** shown.
Put all the files in `mobile/assets/food/` (create the folder), or send them to me in chat, and I'll add them to the app.

## How to make them look the same

1. Paste the **style line** first, then the dish line, as one message. One picture per message.
2. Ask for a **square** picture (1024 × 1024).
3. **Transparent background** is best (ChatGPT: add "transparent background, PNG"). Plain white is fine too.
4. If a picture has text, a logo, a hand, or a brand name on the packaging, ask again.

**Style line (paste this before every dish):**

> Professional food photograph, a single serving seen from a 45-degree angle, centred, the whole plate or bowl visible, soft natural daylight, crisp detail, appetising and realistic, Indian restaurant style, isolated on a transparent background, no text, no logos, no hands, no people, square 1:1.
